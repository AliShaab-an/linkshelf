LinkShelf support

Having trouble parking, finding, or restoring a link? We would like to help.

Contact support

Email ali.shaaban2002@gmail.com with a short description of the problem, your Chrome version, and the LinkShelf version shown in chrome://extensions.

Email support Report an issue on GitHub

Before reporting a problem

  • Reload LinkShelf from chrome://extensions and try the action again.
  • Normal web pages can be parked; Chrome blocks extensions from handling restricted pages such as chrome:// URLs.
  • Check that notifications are enabled in LinkShelf and Windows if a reminder did not appear.
  • Do not include private URLs, notes, exported backup files, passwords, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Your data

LinkShelf has no account or remote server. Its data is stored locally in Chrome. Review the privacy policy for complete details.
